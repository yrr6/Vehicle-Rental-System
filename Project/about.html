About Us

Vehicle Rental System is a modern vehicle rental platform designed to provide reliable, affordable, and convenient transportation services. Our system allows users to rent different types of vehicles including cars, bikes, CNGs, and trucks.

We focus on customer satisfaction by offering flexible booking, transparent fuel cost calculation, and well-maintained vehicles. Our goal is to make vehicle rental simple and accessible for everyone.

This project is developed as an academic project using HTML, CSS, and PHP to demonstrate a complete vehicle rental management system.

Our Mission

To provide safe, affordable, and efficient vehicle rental services.

Our Vision

To become a trusted digital vehicle rental platform in Bangladesh.

Our Values

Trust, transparency, and customer satisfaction.
